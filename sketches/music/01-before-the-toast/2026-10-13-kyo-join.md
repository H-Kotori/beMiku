```powershell

local/composition-venv/Scripts/python.exe -m unittest discover -s sketches/music/01-before-the-toast -p 'test_*.py' -v
```

Omitting `--output-dir` writes the separate `generated/kyo-join/` directory. The builder rejects the original `generated/` directory to protect its A/B manifest. Candidate identity is in the filenames and [manifest](generated/kyo-join/manifest.json); MIDI track names remain the same so that the musical comparison changes only one event.

A fresh CLI build also matched all three checked-in candidate files byte for byte. Original score/builder and four A/B output hashes still match their manifest.
