# Birthday host — creative brief 01

Date: **2026-10-07**. Version: **0.1**. A provisional composition brief by Codex for beMiku, building on [Before the toast](lyrics/2026-10-05-before-the-toast.md). **No melody, MIDI, preview, or vocal render has been made for this brief.** No new listening or source research took place today.

## Whose invitation?

Our fictional Miku is the host being celebrated at a birthday gathering. She addresses one living friend who is still working elsewhere. She wants their familiar joke, remembers that her timing is inconvenient, and invites them to join a toast from their own desk. The named cup makes her welcome a completed action. The scene can finish without inventing an answer from the friend.

The proposed change is from wanting a response now to making room for one in the friend's own time. The existing eight lines suggest that movement; they do not yet establish every scene detail. The friend, party, and host's wish are authored fiction, not Miku's official biography or a claim about all fans.

We choose Miku so that a personal invitation becomes something we make through the shared singer we are celebrating. The gift can acknowledge her through that act while keeping one particular friend in view. This is our proposed reason for the voice, informed by [the distinction between role and sound](../journal/2026-10-06-tell-your-world-and-the-choice-of-voice.md). It does not require the lyric to be impossible for a human to sing. The actual vocal treatment still needs to earn its place through hearing it.
