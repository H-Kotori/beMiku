# Credits and reuse

beMiku is an independent fan project. Hatsune Miku and the other Piapro Characters belong to Crypton Future Media. Songs belong to the people and rights holders behind them. Credit the producer, lyricist, arranger, visual artist, and other collaborators when the source identifies their roles.

Crypton's [information for creators](https://piapro.net/intl/en_for_creators.html), consulted 2026-09-25, distinguishes designated original character illustrations from other creators' music, videos, illustrations, and 3D work. Its character-image license is not blanket permission to reuse those other works. For synthesized vocals in an original musical work, Crypton directs composers to the relevant software's end-user license agreement.

For this repository, that means:

- Publish our original notes and links to the creator's release, rather than copies of their recordings, full lyrics, translations, or artwork.
- Keep purchased voicebanks, activation information, installers, and any private working assets outside version control.
- Record a source, author, applicable license, and permission evidence before adding any third-party asset to a release.
- Check the selected voicebank's actual terms and applicable character-use terms when preparing the final song and artwork. Software availability and terms may change before 2027.

No general reuse license has been chosen for beMiku's own material yet. The owner's invitation to explore and fork is part of the project's spirit; it does not assign other creators' rights to this repository. A future code or document license must identify exactly which original material it covers. The final song, rendered vocals, artwork, and any third-party assets need their own clearly stated treatment.
