**Sharing The World — language beyond the plan**

Study and source-access date: **November 2, 2026**. Draft for parent review.

**Evidence:** Text-only. I directly read KARENT's release record, BIGHEAD's blog archive containing the complete English lyrics and Japanese translation, and VNN's interview. A later retrieval of the individual blog entry failed; the archive supplied the text used here. VNN identifies its interview as translated from Japanese and warns of translation limits. No audio or video was assessed; no listening timestamps are assigned.

**Version and credits:** The reference is the English vocal song on **Sharing The World**, track 1 of the single released **August 31, 2014**, featuring **Hatsune Miku**. KARENT identifies it as the **MIKU EXPO 2014 in LA & NY** theme and credits **apapico** for artwork. This is not a claim about later language versions or live arrangements. [KARENT record](https://karent.jp/album/1599)

BIGHEAD's **May 18, 2015** blog post credits music and lyrics to **BIGHEAD**, vocals to **Hatsune Miku**, sound engineering to **Kei Suzuki**, and illustration to **apapico**. VNN's interview introduction separately names **TANAKA** as the lyric writer. I retain both attributions; the consulted sources do not explain how the individual and project credits relate. The blog post is later than the single, and I have not verified that their audio masters match. [Creator blog archive](https://bighead.hatenablog.com/?page=5), [VNN interview](https://www.vocaloidnews.net/vnn-interview-with-bighead/)

**Question:** Does an English-language invitation define the limits of the community it can reach?

**My textual reading:** The lyric pairs a widely circulating voice with repeated requests to hear what another person thinks or feels. I read sharing here as involving an answer from someone else, rather than reach alone. This is my reading of the posted words. [Creator-posted lyrics](https://bighead.hatenablog.com/?page=5)

**Creator account and reported response:** In Cinzya's **August 15, 2017** interview, BIGHEAD links his English-language songwriting to the Western music he listened to in high school. He describes surprise at Spanish, Chinese and other covers, and reports requests from Mexican fans for a release. These are his recollections in VNN's translation. I did not read those messages or assess the covers; neither establishes what an entire language community thinks. [Interview](https://www.vocaloidnews.net/vnn-interview-with-bighead/)

**What changed:** I initially approached English as a deliberate bridge to a broad audience. His account gives a more personal starting point in musical taste, followed by responses he did not fully anticipate. A song's circulation need not follow its maker's original plan.

**Unresolved question:** What do specific cover makers preserve or change when they carry this invitation into another language? Their own accounts, and identified versions, are still needed.
