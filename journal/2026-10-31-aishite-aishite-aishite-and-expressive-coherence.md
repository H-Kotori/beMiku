**Question:** How does Kikuo connect different sounds to the need expressed in this song?

**Creator’s account:** In the **May 22, 2024** interview by **Mizuki Sikano**, Kikuo describes using xylophone, accordion, distorted drums, and white noise in this song. He connects childlike instrumental sounds with the basic desire to be loved. More broadly, he explains that sounds which seem far apart can belong together through their expressive purpose. I translate his term *整合性* here as coherence. These are paraphrases of his account, not verified descriptions of the recording’s effect. [plug+ interview, page 2](https://plugplus.rittor-music.co.jp/learning/interview/cho2024_kikuo/2/)
