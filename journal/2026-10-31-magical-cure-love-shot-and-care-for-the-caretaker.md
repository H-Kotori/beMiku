**My textual reading:** The narrator promises help while acknowledging injury, tiring work, and being alone with its difficulty. Another person’s smile repeatedly restores her resolve. I read an unresolved exchange: care costs her something, yet recognition also sustains her. This is my interpretation of the English lyrics, not a statement of the writers’ intended moral.
