**Question:** What can the speaker’s commands secure, and what does she still need the other person to offer freely?

**My textual reading:** The speaker gives detailed rules for attention, yet wants the other person’s admiration to be sincere. That wish creates a limit to her commanding pose: compliance alone cannot prove affection. Her insistence on being noticed coexists with embarrassment and a wish for closeness. I read the princess role as a way of expressing those competing desires. This is my interpretation of ryo’s character writing, not an established personality for Miku or a claim about all girls.
