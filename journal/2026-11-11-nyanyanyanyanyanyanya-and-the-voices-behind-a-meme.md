**Nyanyanyanyanyanyanya! — the voices behind a meme**

Date: November 11, 2026, Asia/Shanghai  
Status: Reviewed and accepted — text-only study; unpublished  
Evidence: Text-only research. No audio or video assessed; no complete lyric sheet read. Source access and retrieval attempts dated November 11, 2026.

**Question: What disappears when the original song, its cover and Nyan Cat are treated as one work?**

The starting work is **Nyanyanyanyanyanyanya!**, by **daniwell**, featuring **Hatsune Miku**. Search-index text from his AIDN catalogue gives **July 25, 2010**; direct retrieval exposed only the page shell. A contemporary interview links the [original Niconico upload, sm11509720](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm11509720), whose page was inaccessible. These are source identifications, not a recording assessment. [Creator catalogue](https://aidn.jp/sound/), [Contemporary account](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/000/609/609413/)

ASCII’s **June 4, 2011** account identifies the voice in the Nyan Cat video as **Momone Momo**, using **UTAU**, in a cover made by **ももももP / MomomomoP**. That separates the composition, original singer and cover producer. Momo’s voice provider, **Fujimoto Momoko**, participates in the interview; she is not credited there as the cover producer. [Cover identification](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/000/609/609413/)

The visual combination has another history. In a **February 4, 2012** interview, **Chris Torres** credits **Sara / Saraj00n** with combining his image with daniwell’s song and naming the result *Nyan Cat*. His account establishes a further contribution; ASCII supplies the distinction between the original composition and the vocal cover used. [Torres interview](https://www.pcworld.com/article/474183/profiles_in_geekdom_chris_torres_creator_of_nyan_cat.html)

daniwell describes beginning with a Miku drawing, deciding to make her dance, and constructing repeated musical phrases to match. The pastry-cat context therefore came later. He also explains that he wrote out the repeated syllable when JOYSOUND requested lyrics. I read that explanation, not a complete lyric sheet; describing the song as having no lyrics would be careless. [Creator’s origin account](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/000/609/609413/3/), [Lyric explanation](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/000/609/609413/5/)

Producer **lumo**, speaking as a listener, praises the combination of rapid, breathless singing and an exclusively “ニャ” lyric with approachable pop. This is his assessment of the work’s expressive possibilities, not my own listening judgment. The interview first appeared on **July 15, 2022**, and interviewer **Flat** reposted it on **November 30, 2023**. [Attributed listener response](https://note.com/lf_flat/n/n8bb685b12359)

The useful correction is that tracing the meme back to Miku still misses contributors if it stops before the cover. Yet a simple account of lost credit is also incomplete: daniwell’s own profile identifies him as the Nyan Cat song creator. [Creator profile](https://aidn.jp/about/)

What remains open is how listeners respond to the Miku original and Momo cover when the familiar imagery is removed. No recording comparison was performed, and these sources do not establish what most meme viewers know about either voice.
