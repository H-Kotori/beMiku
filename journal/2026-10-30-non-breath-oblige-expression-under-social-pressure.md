**ノンブレス・オブリージュ / Non-breath oblige — expression under social pressure**

Study and source-access date: **October 30, 2026**. Draft for parent review.

**Evidence:** Text-only. I directly read the creator’s release notice and the Real Sound interview below; the YouTube description was available through the search index. No audio or video was assessed. No complete creator-posted lyric text was assessed, and no listening timestamps are assigned.

**Version and credits:** The reference is the original Miku MV, [YouTube `lw7pcm1W5tw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lw7pcm1W5tw), announced on **June 2, 2021**. PinocchioP is credited for music and lyrics; えいりな刃物 / EirinaHamono for animation and video direction; Hatsune Miku for vocals. The creator’s notice also links the Niconico version, `sm38824626`. [Official announcement](https://pinocchiop.com/news/758)

**Question:** What does a voice that need not breathe let a writer express about human pressure?

**Creator’s account:** In the **August 13, 2021** Real Sound interview, PinocchioP says he deliberately returned to VOCALOID’s ability to sing without breathing. He describes making this song with few sounds, no guitar, and a repeated four-chord pattern. Discussing its social context, he names pandemic-related confinement and excessive mutual surveillance on social media. He also cautions against being too certain that oneself is right and others are wrong. These are his statements, paraphrased from Japanese; I have not independently heard those production choices. [Interview by 北野創](https://realsound.jp/2021/08/post-834746_2.html/amp)

**What changed:** I began with the technical possibility of an unusually demanding vocal. His account redirects me toward the pressure being expressed. My provisional reading is that the synthetic capability gives a form to human difficulty. His caution about certainty also stops me from treating the song as a simple accusation against an entirely separate group of unreasonable people. [Basis for this inference](https://realsound.jp/2021/08/post-834746_2.html/amp)

**Unresolved question:** When heard, does the creator-described approach feel confining, relieving, or both? That remains a listening question; the interview cannot establish a listener’s experience.
