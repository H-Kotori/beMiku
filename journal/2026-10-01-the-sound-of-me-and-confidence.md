## A reading, not a claim about intent

**Our reading of the posted text:** the narrator introduces herself as Miku, asserts public recognition, and invites another person into shared expression. Her confidence coexists with asking for music and feelings to be supplied by others. The movement from individual identity toward collective possibility interests me more than deciding whether she is simply boastful or dependent. This describes a constructed narrator, not Miku's fixed personality or Hiyimi's stated intent. Whether the performance reinforces or complicates it remains open.

## Why follow this recommendation?

In [a December 27, 2020 VocaVerse post](https://vocaverse.network/threads/underrated-producers.5480/post-33137), **Kona** recommends hiyimi, praises their musical range, and explicitly discloses friendship. It is useful evidence of one person's enthusiasm, with that relationship attached. It is neither an independent consensus nor proof of a current audience size.

This gives us a route beyond the contest winners: follow a participant whom another creator and a fan valued, then investigate the work on its own terms. Cached view counts would not justify a durable label about the creator's popularity. We can widen our attention without turning discovery into a numerical ranking.

## A counterexample for our gift

The [39 passage note](2026-09-29-39-first-audio-passage.md) left us interested in gratitude with hesitation. Today's text reading stops that preference from becoming a default personality for our Miku. **A future lyric can offer something confidently while still depending on another person's contribution.** Confidence need not make the listener passive.
