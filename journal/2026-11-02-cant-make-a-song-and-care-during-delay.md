**Question:** What does encouragement ask of someone who keeps failing to finish?

**My textual reading:** The singer is impatient but concerned about the producer's sleep and health. Completion leads to an invitation to try again. I read encouragement as caring for the person throughout the process. This fictional relationship does not establish the creator's daily routine.
