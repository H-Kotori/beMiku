**Question:** Can knowing that a performance is constructed become part of participating in it?

**My textual reading:** The lyrics repeatedly question the benefit of separating scripts from sincerity and real things from constructed ones. They locate enjoyment in an ambiguous space, include a self-referential assertion about Miku singing by her own will, and end with a fiction notice. I read that assertion as part of the song’s theatrical argument, not evidence of software autonomy. My provisional interpretation is that knowledge of construction need not cancel meaning. [Creator-posted lyrics](https://piapro.jp/t/Uh0A)

**One listener’s response:** In a **December 6, 2020** essay about the Osaka opening performance of Magical Mirai 2020, **口閉** explicitly connects the song’s theatrical premise with spectators responding to Miku onstage. This is one person’s reading of a concert, not evidence about the selected studio recording or audience consensus. [Listener essay](https://note.com/stonepaddyki/n/n64f40046ac4f)

**What changed:** My question has shifted from whether the illusion is convincing to how awareness and participation might coexist.
