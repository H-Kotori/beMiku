# Mitchie M: what are we calling natural?

Date and source access: **2026-10-11**. Evidence: two direct creator interviews and creator/official publication notices. **No audio or video was assessed, no complete lyrics were read, and no new listener testimony was collected.**

Today's question: **can natural phrasing be a useful goal without treating realism as the whole purpose of Miku's voice?** We approach *好き！雪！本気マジック* as a future listening reference, while keeping Mitchie M's general working methods separate from this particular recording.

## Identify the reference

[Crypton's December 26, 2013 announcement](https://blog.piapro.net/2013/12/snowmiku2014mitchie-m.html) identifies the SNOW MIKU 2014 event song as Mitchie M's work and explicitly introduces a chorus excerpt. His [February 1, 2014 MV announcement](https://mitchie-m.com/information/snow-miku-2014公式テーマ曲『好き！雪！本気マジック』のmv/) credits **llcheesell and CatsAX** for video and **小森のぐ** for illustration. It links the [original Miku music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=79N1O0lF0GY). The MV announcement date is not the first public appearance of the music.

These pages establish attribution and presentation. Their promotional enthusiasm is not our listening response. We did not verify a song-specific account of its vocal production.

## What the creator describes

In [Yamaha's May 19, 2022 interview](https://www.vocaloid.com/creator/creator_002/), Mitchie M describes adapting a pre-existing song upward because its register did not suit the Miku voice he was using. His workflow combines detailed editor work with subsequent audio editing. He also prizes Miku's clear high register, including notes he says are difficult for human singers to produce. These are his accounts and preferences, not universal range specifications or measurements of this song.

In [SakuMag's August 3, 2023 interview](https://sakumaga.sakura.ad.jp/entry/mitchie_m/), he gives practical examples of pursuing humanlike delivery: placing breaths appropriately, adjusting vocal quality and strength with register, and adding an intermediate vowel when moving between particular vowel sounds. The point for us is the attention to transitions. This is a description of his practice, not a recipe we have tested or evidence that every example occurs in *好き！雪！本気マジック*.

## Our interpretation and a constraint for the sketch

His interest in humanlike articulation coexists with affection for qualities he finds distinctive in Miku. **Our inference:** intelligible words, convincing phrase movement, and how obviously synthetic a voice sounds deserve separate judgments. A performance can succeed on one and still need work on another. The [earlier kz interview study](2026-10-06-tell-your-world-and-the-choice-of-voice.md) supplies a useful contrast in stated preferences: making synthesis conspicuous can also be an artistic choice. We have not made an audible comparison between these producers.
