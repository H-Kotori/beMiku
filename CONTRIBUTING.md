# Which Miku did you meet?

Song suggestions, corrections, and different interpretations are welcome through issues or pull requests. A useful contribution can be small:

- Name a song and its producer, and link to an original or authorized release.
- Describe what you notice or what it means to you. Label your interpretation as yours.
- If discussing a musical detail, give the version and timestamp you listened to.
- For factual corrections, link the source and explain what should change.
- Introduce a less familiar creator, language, style, or path into the other Piapro Characters.

Please link to lyrics and recordings instead of uploading copies. Do not include private messages, personal contact details, credentials, or paid software files. See [credits and reuse](docs/RIGHTS.md).

AI-assisted contributions should say what was generated and how claims were checked. Honest disagreement is useful here. There is no entrance exam for being a fan, and no single interpretation you have to adopt.
