# A first listening map

Research date: **2026-09-25**. Status: **links and metadata researched; audio not yet assessed**.

This is a starting queue, not a ranking or a claim to represent every Miku community. It gives us several ways into the question behind beMiku: how can one voice carry so many different people? The questions below are proposed listening prompts, not findings from listening and not statements of the creators' intentions.

Credits refer to the linked version. An upload, an album release, a remake, and a concert performance can have different dates and personnel. Dates below identify their source event; they should not silently become an "original release year." Links point to creator, label, or official event sources. No music, artwork, or lyrics are copied into this repository.

## Twelve starting points

### 1. メルト / Melt — ryo (supercell)

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [original Niconico upload](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm1715919), [supercell's official biography](https://www.supercell.jp/biography.html).
- **Verified context:** The official biography places the Niconico posting in its 2007 section and credits ryo for production, Hatsune Miku for vocals, and 119 for illustration. The original upload did not render in the research tool; the official biography supplies the credit evidence.
- **Listening question — early songwriting:** When do we hear Miku as a recognizable character, and when do we hear the particular person whose story the songwriter gives her?

### 2. ローリンガール / Rollin Girl — wowaka

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Source:** [official video on the hitorie / wowaka channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vnw8zURAxkU).
- **Verified context:** The official video's title identifies both wowaka and Hatsune Miku. This is a later YouTube presentation of an older work; its upload date should not be used as the song's original release date.
- **Listening question — urgency and difficult emotion:** What do repetition, phrasing, and musical momentum make us feel, and which meanings are supported by the work rather than supplied by a popular fan explanation?

### 3. glow — keeno

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku Dark, as named by the creator.
- **Source:** [keeno's original Piapro page](https://piapro.jp/t/lcNu).
- **Verified context:** The page credits keeno for music and words and records a Piapro posting on 2010-06-28. It explicitly names the Dark voice in the title.
- **Listening question — intimacy:** How much emotional weight can vocal color, space, and a restrained phrase carry? Which details would still matter without an image of Miku?

### 4. Tell Your World — livetune

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [official music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PqJNc9KVIZE), [TOY'S FACTORY release page](https://www.toysfactory.co.jp/sp/artist/livetune/disco/896).
- **Verified context:** The label credits livetune feat. Hatsune Miku and dates the *Tell Your World EP* to 2012-03-14. That is the EP date, not a claim about the song's first public appearance.
- **Listening question — electronic pop and connection:** How does a song move from one person's voice toward the feeling of people finding one another? What musical or visual choices make that happen?

### 5. 39 — sasakure.UK × DECO*27

- **Featured vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [official music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ecU_uJRzhfk), [label's SoundCloud upload](https://soundcloud.com/umaainc/39-1), [U/M/A/A anniversary context](https://store.umaa.net/items/1124712).
- **Verified context:** The label describes this collaboration as a song for Miku's fifth anniversary. The listed vocal is the featured singer credit, not an assertion that no human backing voices appear.
- **Listening question — giving thanks:** Who is thanking whom: Miku, the producers, the listeners, or all of them? How could our birthday gift acknowledge others without making gratitude feel generic?

### 6. 愛して 愛して 愛して / Aishite Aishite Aishite — Kikuo

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [Kikuo's music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTrm_idbhUk), [KARENT: Kikuo Miku 3](https://karent.jp/album/1357).
- **Verified context:** KARENT lists this song with Hatsune Miku on an album released digitally on 2013-09-11. The video is a separate publication; its later date does not make the composition new.
- **Listening question — unsettling expression:** What happens when a voice associated with cuteness carries need, unease, or obsession? Can we distinguish the narrator, Miku's instrument-like role, and the creator instead of treating them as one person?

### 7. Miku — Anamanaguchi

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [lyric video on the official Hatsune Miku channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NocXEwsJGOQ), [Anamanaguchi's own track page](https://anamanaguchi.bandcamp.com/track/miku-feat-hatsune-miku).
- **Verified context:** The official channel calls it a collaboration between Anamanaguchi and Hatsune Miku; that video was posted on 2016-05-27.
- **Listening question — international identity:** What makes a self-introduction work for someone who knows almost nothing about Miku? Does introducing her name also introduce the people making her possible?

### 8. 砂の惑星 / DUNE — Hachi

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AS4q9yaWJkI), [Hachi / Kenshi Yonezu's official announcement](https://reissuerecords.net/2017/07/21/%E3%83%8F%E3%83%81%E3%80%8E%E7%A0%82%E3%81%AE%E6%83%91%E6%98%9F%E3%80%8Fmv%E5%85%AC%E9%96%8B%E6%98%A0%E5%83%8F%E5%88%B6%E4%BD%9C%E3%81%AF%E3%80%8C%E5%8D%97%E6%96%B9%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E6%89%80/).
- **Verified context:** The 2017-07-21 announcement identifies the work as part of *Magical Mirai 2017*'s official album and credits Minakata Laboratory for the video. The linked video is the Miku version; a creator's self-cover is a different performance.
- **Listening question — community looking at itself:** Can a celebration also question the condition of the creative scene? Which readings come from the work, which from a creator interview, and which from subsequent fan debate?

### 9. ハイパーリアリティショウ / Hyper Reality Show — Utsu-P

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [creator's Bandcamp track](https://utsup.bandcamp.com/track/--91), [officially distributed audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=meeo7NwW7rE), [KARENT: RENAISSANCE](https://karent.jp/album/2483).
- **Verified context:** KARENT credits Utsu-P and Hatsune Miku and classifies the album as rock. Bandcamp dates that album to 2019-08-12; KARENT's digital release date is 2019-10-16. Those are distinct release records.
- **Listening question — rock and the reality of performance:** What lets an obviously constructed performance feel meaningful? How does this version of Miku expand the vocal textures we would consider for our own song?

### 10. MikuFiesta — AlexTrip Sands

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [official MIKU EXPO contest result and embedded song](https://mikuexpo.com/5thcontest/), [the official video embedded there](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TMdHGvrwyJc).
- **Verified context:** The official result names AlexTrip Sands as the grand-prize winner of the fifth-anniversary song contest. Its entry rules required vocals made with Hatsune Miku software and allowed any language; the stated prize was performance at the 2020 Europe concerts. The contest's submission period was October 2019, so "2020 concert song" is not an original upload date.
- **Listening question — language and local belonging:** Investigate the language and rhythmic traditions behind this celebration. How can a creator make Miku feel at home in their own community without needing to reproduce somebody else's idea of her?

### 11. ノンブレス・オブリージュ / Non-breath oblige — PinocchioP

- **Vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [PinocchioP's official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lw7pcm1W5tw), [creator's album page](https://pinocchiop.com/discography/744).
- **Verified context:** The video, published on 2021-06-02, explicitly credits PinocchioP for music and lyrics, EirinaHamono for animation and video direction, and Hatsune Miku for vocals.
- **Listening question — synthetic capability as expression:** How can breath, its absence, and the density of words become part of a song's meaning? What should remain physically singable in our gift, and what might deliberately use a synthesized voice's different possibilities?

### 12. M@GICAL☆CURE! LOVE ♥ SHOT! — SAWTOWNE

- **Featured vocal:** Hatsune Miku.
- **Sources:** [SAWTOWNE's official music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LaEgpNBt-bQ), [official MIKU EXPO contest result](https://mikuexpo.com/10th/contest_jp.html), [official contest-winner upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiyUwuieWjo).
- **Verified context:** The MIKU EXPO tenth-anniversary contest names SAWTOWNE as its grand-prize winner. The official contest upload appeared on 2024-02-15 and the creator's MV on 2024-03-22; these are publication dates for those versions. MIKU EXPO's tenth anniversary is distinct from Miku's birthday anniversary. The creator's MV and contest entry should be logged separately when comparing their credits or audience chants.
- **Listening question — participation:** What turns a catchy phrase into something an audience wants to answer together? Could our gift leave room for listeners to participate rather than only observe?

## How to turn this queue into understanding

Take a small number of songs at a time. For each, record the exact version, what was actually heard, a few timestamps, what is still unclear, and one thing worth returning to. Put sourced creator comments and individual fans' interpretations beside those observations, with links and clear attribution. A fan's reading can be valuable without being the creator's intended meaning or a consensus.

This first map still needs smaller creators, more languages, and perspectives outside highly visible concert and internet favorites. Future discoveries should be allowed to change the questions, not just enlarge the list. We will be ready to write our own song when we can explain what we want to say through Miku and why particular musical choices serve it, not when we have reached an arbitrary listening count.
